Added a methodological distinction between historically/materially grounded explanation and the Manifesto’s stronger deterministic prediction that bourgeois fall and proletarian victory are “equally inevitable,” so `scientific` is not used as an editorial certificate of empirical truth. Corrected the first French translation from 1851 to before the June 1848 insurrection and retained the 1850 Helen Macfarlane *Red Republican* translation as the first English version, following Marx and Engels’s later prefaces. Replaced the conversion-era numbered citation lattice and low-quality forum/PDF links with a compact named source architecture centered on Engels’s *Principles of Communism*, the *Manifesto* and its authorial prefaces, Engels’s *Socialism: Utopian and Scientific*, Marx’s 1880 introduction, and modern scholarly context from the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy.
