# Academic editorial profile

The repository-wide editorial rules now live in [`EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md`](EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md).

`academic` is one editorial profile rather than the default voice for every artifact. It applies to research reports, research briefs, technical histories, literature reviews, analytical notes, reference articles, and other material intended to read as impersonal scholarly prose.

The academic profile requires, subject to legitimate quotations and rare methodological necessity:

- impersonal or third-person narration;
- no second-person address;
- removal of accidental first-person narration;
- removal of prompt/user/assistant/conversation residue;
- removal of assignment-stage framing such as `testing out a hypothesis` when the proposition can instead be stated and evaluated directly;
- avoidance of document self-reference such as `this report examines` where the substantive claim can be stated directly;
- clean grammar, spelling, citations, links, assets, diagrams, headings, and metadata.

Do **not** apply these perspective rules blindly to artifacts declared `argumentative`, `stylized`, `personal`, or `creative`. Universal integrity rules still apply to every profile.
